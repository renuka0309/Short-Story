About the author

Renuka Ullegaddi

I am a writer who loves stories about family, silence and the small, quiet ways that society changes. Not Unloved is my story of three sisters who grew up with every comfort, yet never felt close to their father, and of how patriarchy shapes love inside a home.

I wrote it because many children are told they are loved without ever feeling it. I wanted to show that care and closeness are not the same thing, and that change, though slow, is happening across generations.
